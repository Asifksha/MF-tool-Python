# MF-tool-Python :

mftool is a library for getting real time Mutual Funds data in India. It can be used in various types of projects which requires getting live quotes for a given mutual fund scheme or build large data sets for further data analytics.

What is Mutual Fund:

Mutual Funds pool money from investors and then invest them in a basket of securities such as stocks, bonds, debt, etc. An investor can invest in a Mutual Fund by buying its units and can earn a profit/loss by selling these units at a higher/lower price. The price of these units is called NAV(Net Asset Value) and is determined by the AUM(Assets under Management) and the units issued.

NAV in mutual funds:

NAV stands for Net Asset Value, which is a key metric in mutual funds. It represents the per-unit price of a mutual fund's assets after subtracting its liabilities. In simpler terms, it's the value of one share or unit of a mutual fund at a given time.

The NAV is calculated daily (usually after the market closes) and reflects the total value of the fund's holdings (stocks, bonds, or other assets) divided by the number of outstanding units or shares.

NAV is important because it helps investors track the performance of their investment in a mutual fund and determines the buying and selling price of fund units.
